Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Metodix ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application, the sign-in and consent pages, and the Metodix connector for AI assistants.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account (in the App or on our web sign-up page) or sign in with Google, we collect your email address and display name.

Workout Data: The App stores data you voluntarily enter, including:

  • Training programs and exercises
  • Workout sessions and sets (weights, reps, duration)
  • Client profiles (if you use the trainer features)
  • Progress notes

Product Analytics: The App sends usage events (for example: app opened, account created, client added, workout logged) and app error reports to PostHog, hosted in the EU. These events are linked to your account ID and are used only to understand how the App is used and to fix problems. They do not include the contents of your workouts or your clients' data. Feedback messages you choose to send from the App, and the Google Play install referrer, are sent the same way. We do not collect location data or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • To store and display your workout history and progress
  • To enable trainer-client relationships within the App

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure with row-level security policies ensuring that each user can only access their own data. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method (Google Privacy Policy)
  • PostHog (EU) — product analytics and error reports (PostHog Privacy Policy)
  • Resend — delivery of account emails such as confirmation codes (Resend Privacy Policy)

We do not use advertising SDKs.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Your workout data is private and accessible only to you (and your trainer/clients if you use group features).

5a. AI Assistants (Metodix Connector)

You can connect an AI assistant you use (for example, ChatGPT or Claude) to your Metodix account. Nothing is shared until you sign in on our consent page and press "Allow". After that, the assistant can — only when you ask it to — search your clients and exercises, read workout history and progress, and log or plan workouts on your behalf. The data the assistant reads is passed to that assistant's provider and is handled under their privacy policy. We do not receive or store your conversations with the assistant; we only process the requests needed to read and write your training log. You can revoke access at any time in the App under Settings → Agents.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time on the account deletion page or by contacting us at the email below. Upon request, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
